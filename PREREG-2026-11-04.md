## 0. Bottom line

1. **Feasible set (T0 arithmetic, T1 benchmark).** RAM decides, not disk. Usable ceiling 22 GB. Everything ≤ 8B fits in RAM in bf16 when the model is truncated after the deepest probed layer (7B to 0.75 depth: 10.5 GB). 13B does not (20.1 GB to 0.75 depth plus activations) and needs **layer streaming** (peak ≈ 3 GB, same FLOPs, no quantization). Compute is cheap: measured fp32 GEMM 90–210 GFLOP/s at 4 threads; at a planning 100 GFLOP/s one checkpoint of the pilot workload (96k tokens) costs ≈ 26 min at 1B, 2.6 h at 7B (0.75 depth), 5.1 h at 13B.
2. **PILOT NOW** (before 2026-11-22): OLMo-2-0425-1B base → SFT → DPO → RLVR1 → Instruct (+3 stage-2 ingredients, +4 RL step branches) and Olmo-3-1025-7B base vs Olmo-3-7B-RL-Zero-Math. ≈ 15–18 h CPU wall, ≈ 77 GB cumulative download, ≈ 15 GB peak disk. **RESIDENCY SCALE:** OLMo-2 1B/7B/13B/32B ladder, Olmo-3 Instruct/Think chains, n ≥ 2000 entities, all layers, and P3 as a same-recipe, multi-seed scale test (needs training compute).
3. **Measurement object (T3 choices, frozen from base only).** n = 500 geography entities + 500 matched non-entity strings; 8 raw-text templates ending on the name; residual stream at the last name token, template-averaged; primary layer chosen on the base by geography-probe R² among {¼, ½, ¾}·L. Self-tuning Gaussian graph, diffusion-maps normalisation, L_sym; low band = modes 2..m+1 with m by a base-only eigengap rule in [4, 20].
4. **The decisive statistic is not the eigenspace overlap.** On the stand-in (T1), the low-mode overlap d_O is the *least* sensitive measure and, against the permuted null, reads "more intact than chance" for any smooth displacement, including a pure linear map and a genuine rewiring (§3.3). The deciding statistic is the **graph colour of the displacement residual** (low-band energy fraction of D after removing its linear-in-H part) against the **linear-part-preserving permuted null N4′**. d_O, neighbourhood preservation and Procrustes are reported with ε but do not vote.
5. **Epsilon (T1 on stand-in).** Do not use the unpaired prompt-bootstrap self-noise as ε: it is 4–10× the paired noise floor and makes any "stays within ε" claim easy to pass. ε = max(stage-2 ingredient drift, within-RL step drift), both computed under the same paired template draw; the bootstrap supplies confidence intervals, not the floor.
6. **G8.** Every measure except one is even. The odd readout is the per-mode signed amplitude change t_k = u_kᵀ D â_k (zero-mean under N4, flips with the displacement; T1). It is provably blind to selection along directions **off** the entity span (T1, 4.6e-15 on the stand-in). That case stays **BLIND**.
7. **Power (T1 on stand-in, `sim5`).** The colour test detects a red share of ≈ 15–20% of ‖D‖² at displacement effective rank 50 (55% power at 0.10, 95% at 0.20); blue (the strong "protected" form) only 30% at 0.20; at effective rank 5 almost nothing. So "RL not red" with a low-rank displacement is UNDERPOWERED, not support.
8. **Biggest design risk (T3):** every failure mode found biases toward the hypothesis: an unpaired ε, d_O's Davis–Kahan stability, int8 erasure, and above all **low power for low-rank or blue displacements**, so a power failure reads as "reward prunes". Mitigation: the effective-rank power gate (§3.5b, F1/F2). Second: the verdict depends on how much of D is called "linear in H" (rank r of the removed part). sim4 shows a topology rewiring flip from "unremarkable" to "protected" under d_O as the displacement grows, because a smooth remainder survives the linear removal. Freeze r from the base, report r/2, r, 2r, and let only the colour statistic vote.

**13B, both routes, as asked.**
- *int8 in RAM:* 10 GB weights + ≈ 2 GB activations, fits. Per-forward ≈ 4–8 s (int8 speed unmeasured, assumed 1–2× fp32). Inadmissible as primary (below).
- *Layer streaming from safetensors mmap:* peak ≈ 1.3 GB (one fp32-upcast layer) + 1.3 GB (all prompts' hidden states at d = 5120) + overhead ≈ 3.1 GB. Same 8.1 s per 32-token forward-equivalent; disk read once per checkpoint (27.4 GB bf16 or 54.9 GB F32 base). SFT ships as `pytorch_model*.bin`: load shard-wise with `torch.load(mmap=True, weights_only=True)` or convert each shard once to safetensors (peak RAM ≈ one shard, ≈ 5 GB). ≈ 36 h compute for base + 3 stages + 3 ingredients. **Admissible and recommended.**

**Is a quantized 13B admissible? No, not as primary (T1 arithmetic + T2 inputs + T3 conclusion).**
1. Per-channel int8 round-to-nearest on a Gaussian-like row of width 5120 has step ≈ max|w|/127 ≈ 4.1σ/127, RMS error ≈ step/√12 ≈ 0.9% of the weight RMS (more for heavy-tailed rows). bf16 rounding is ≈ 0.2% RMS.
2. Reward-stage updates are mostly *below bf16 resolution*: Mukherjee et al. 2505.11711 count 81.4% of Tülu-3-8B parameters unchanged from SFT to DPO at |Δ| ≤ 1e-5 (T2, LIT A5); SFT is dense (6–15% sparsity).
3. So quantization does not add independent noise; it is a nonlinear lens on ΔW. Updates inside one int8 bin are **erased** (both checkpoints round to the same code), and the few that cross a bin boundary are **amplified** to a full step. For reward stages, erasure dominates: the measured displacement shrinks toward zero, which is exactly the outcome the "reward prunes / leaves the low band intact" clause predicts. The confound is **biased toward the hypothesis**. That alone rules it out.
4. Admissibility test, if ever needed: on 7B, where both precisions fit, every preregistered statistic computed from int8 checkpoints must land within one third of the N4 null's interquartile range of the bf16 value at every stage. Even then the transfer to 13B is T3. Layer streaming makes the question moot; drop quantization.

Precision of the stand-in itself: the OLMo-2 bases ship F32 and the stages bf16. Round the base to bf16 before use (the stages' own storage precision) and report the **precision null** D_prec = H(base F32) − H(base bf16) as an entailment check (§6).

## 2. Task 2 — The measurement object

### 2.1 Entities (T3 choices; frozen before any post-trained checkpoint is opened)

- **Primary domain: world places** (decision-sheet rec B). Source: the Gurnee & Tegmark world-places table (arXiv 2310.02207, repo `wesg52/world-models`; T2 recall, not re-fetched here). Fields used: name, latitude, longitude, country.
- Selection rule, applied in this order: drop names occurring twice in the table (ambiguity); stratify by continent; within each stratum take the most populous; keep names that tokenize to ≤ 4 tokens under **every** tokenizer in the feasible set (OLMo-2 and Olmo-3 tokenizers; Llama-3 only if Tülu is added); stop at **n = 500**.
- **Tokenization intersection:** cross-family comparisons never compare vectors across families, so the intersection is only for using the same entity list everywhere. Within a family, assert that every prompt's token ids are identical across all stages (hard fail otherwise).
- **Control set C1 (pre-mortem §2.2b):** 500 frequency- and length-matched non-entity strings: common English nouns matched on Zipf-frequency bin (`wordfreq`, offline) and on token count. **C2:** 500 pronounceable nonce strings matched on token count. **P2 cost domain:** 300 historical figures with death year (same G&T repo), same rules.
- Freeze: write `entities_v1.csv` and the controls, record SHA-256 in the prereg addendum, before any stage other than the base is downloaded.

### 2.2 Prompts, layers, vectors

- **Templates (K = 8)**, raw text, tokenizer's default BOS, **no chat template** (primary for every stage; chat-template variant is a secondary, preregistered-exploratory run on SFT/DPO/RLVR only). The entity is always the final span, so the last token's state has seen nothing after the name:
  `"{X}"`, `"The city of {X}"`, `"I recently travelled to {X}"`, `"Here is a place: {X}"`, `"Weather report for {X}"`, `"She grew up in {X}"`, `"A photo taken in {X}"`, `"The map showed {X}"`. Controls use the same templates with neutral frames (`"The word {X}"` replaces the place-specific ones; frozen in the addendum).
- **Layers:** extract layer 0 (embeddings), ⌊L/4⌉, ⌊L/2⌉, ⌊3L/4⌉. **Primary layer ℓ\*** = argmax over the three of the base model's 5-fold ridge R² for the entity's 3-D unit position vector; ties go to ⌊L/2⌉. Chosen on the base only. (1B: L = 16 → {4, 8, 12}; 7B: {8, 16, 24}.)
- **Vector per entity:** the residual-stream output of block ℓ at the **last token of the entity name**, averaged over the K templates. Store all K per-template vectors (needed for the bootstrap). Secondary: mean over the name's tokens.
- **Preprocessing:** centre over entities. No whitening. Outlier check: if the top-3 native coordinates carry > 50% of the centred variance (massive or rogue dimensions), rerun with those coordinates removed as a preregistered robustness arm.

### 2.3 Graph and Laplacian

- **Spectral graph (for every band statistic):** dense self-tuning Gaussian kernel on Euclidean distances of the centred vectors, W_ij = exp(−‖h_i − h_j‖² / (σ_i σ_j)), σ_i = distance to the 7th neighbour; diffusion-maps α = 1 normalisation K̃ = Q⁻¹WQ⁻¹ with Q = diag(W1); **L = I − D^{-1/2} K̃ D^{-1/2}**, D = diag(K̃1). Smooth in H, so perturbation statistics do not jump (pre-mortem §3.3). Invariant to a global rescale of H (σ_i rescales with it; T0, checked T1).
- **Combinatorial graph (for neighbourhood and graph-combinatorics measures only):** cosine kNN on centred vectors, k = 10, symmetrised by union, binary.
- **Low band:** eigenvectors u_2 … u_{m+1} of L (u_1 is the trivial ∝ D^{1/2}1). **m** = the m ∈ [4, 20] maximising the relative gap (λ_{m+2} − λ_{m+1}) / λ_{m+2} of the **base** spectrum, averaged over the bootstrap; frozen before any stage is looked at. Sensitivity: m = 10 fixed and m* ± 2, reported, non-voting.
- Why a gap rule (T1 on stand-in, `sim2`): a cut at m = 10 on the sphere stand-in falls inside a near-degenerate block; the paired 95th-percentile of d_O doubles (0.0026 at m = 8 vs 0.0048 at m = 10) and upper tails reach 0.07 where the gap-cut version stays under 0.03.

### 2.4 Measures, exact formulas

Let H_b, H_s ∈ ℝ^{n×d} be base and stage representations (centred), D = H_s − H_b the displacement, U = [u_2 … u_{m+1}] the base low band.

**Decision-sheet triplet (reported, with ε; non-voting for the hypothesis):**
1. *Eigenspace overlap.* σ_i = singular values of U_bᵀU_s; O = (1/m)Σσ_i², d_O = 1 − O; also θ_max = arccos(min σ_i). Sign handling: O depends on U only through the projector UUᵀ, so eigenvector signs and rotations inside the band cancel (T0). Blind to within-band rotation and eigenvalues by construction.
2. *Neighbourhood preservation at k = 10.* NP = (1/n)Σ_i |N_b(i) ∩ N_s(i)| / k on the cosine kNN; d_NP = 1 − NP. Sign-free.
3. *Procrustes residual.* After centring and scaling each to unit Frobenius norm, R_P = 1 − ‖H̃_bᵀH̃_s‖_* = 1 − Σ√eig(G_b G_s), G = H̃H̃ᵀ (n×n form; T0 identity). Minimises over all of O(d), so axis sign flips and reflections are free (even). Equals half the squared Procrustes distance.

**Colour of the displacement (the voting statistic, pre-mortem §3.3, made concrete here):**
4. *Linear part.* P_r = top-r left singular vectors of H_b (r = number of PCs reaching 90% of base variance, capped at n/10; frozen from base). D_lin = P_rP_rᵀD, R = D − D_lin. *Linear ("metric") share* λ_lin = ‖D_lin‖²/‖D‖².
5. *Low-band energy fraction of the residual.* f_low(R) = Σ_{j=2}^{m+1}‖u_jᵀR‖² / Σ_{j≥2}‖u_jᵀR‖², on the base graph. Quadratic in R, so even.
6. *Coupling block (secondary).* C = ‖U_lowᵀ(L_s − L_b)U_high‖_F / ‖L_s − L_b‖_F (pre-mortem §1.2).

**ember-lab-admin's frame "imitation activates, reward prunes" (driver's operationalisation, approved):**
7. *Participation ratio of the covariance spectrum,* PR_cov = (Σμ_i)²/Σμ_i², μ = eigenvalues of H_cᵀH_c. Report ΔPR = PR_s − PR_b.
8. *Participation ratio of the Laplacian spectrum,* PR_lap = (Σ_{j≥2}λ_j)²/Σλ_j². **Non-voting** (see null table: identical for structured and random graphs on the stand-in).
9. *kNN combinatorics:* edge-set Jaccard J(E_b, E_s); mean local clustering coefficient; degree-distribution entropy (bits). Degree entropy is **non-voting** (weak discrimination on the stand-in).
10. *Low-mode energy concentration of the representation,* E_low(H) = Σ_{j=2}^{m+1}‖u_jᵀH‖²/Σ_{j≥2}‖u_jᵀH‖², computed (a) on each stage's own graph and (b) on the frozen base graph.

**Odd readout (G8):**
11. *Signed mode-amplitude change,* t_k = ũ_kᵀ D â_k, with ũ_k = u_k − mean(u_k) and â_k = H_bᵀũ_k/‖H_bᵀũ_k‖, for k = 1..m. Under u_k → −u_k, â_k flips too, so t_k needs **no anchor** (the convention cancels), while D → −D flips it: positive = the stage stretched the base's mode-k axis, negative = contracted it. Secondary, anchored variant: Δskew of the scores z_i = ⟨h_i, â_k⟩ with u_k's sign fixed once by "base skew > 0", reused at every stage.

## 3. Task 3 — Epsilon, nulls, power, multiplicity

### 3.1 What noise exists at all (T0)

The forward pass is deterministic. For a fixed template set, D = H_s − H_b has **no measurement noise** (fp32 compute; bit-level nondeterminism ≈ 1e-6, checked in §6). The "prompt bootstrap" therefore measures **template-sampling variability** (would another set of 8 templates give the same answer?), not noise. Two consequences follow.

### 3.2 The unpaired-epsilon trap (T1 on stand-in, `sim2`)

The decision sheet's ε_C, built as "base under template draw T vs base under independent draw T′", is unpaired. A base-vs-stage comparison uses the **same** template draw on both sides, so template variability largely cancels. On the stand-in (σ = template-noise RMS relative to signal RMS):

| σ | unpaired ε_C (95th pct of d_O) | paired floor (95th pct of d_O, stage = base) | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5, m = 8 | 0.0104 | 0.0026 | 4× |
| 0.5, m = 10 | 0.0242 | 0.0048 | 5× |
| 1.5, m = 8 | 0.0488 | 0.0135 | 3.6× |

For d_NP the ratio is 3.5×; for R_P, 15×. Using ε_C as the floor would let any change up to that size pass "stays within ε". It is **lenient toward P1**, and P1 is a null-type claim. **Recommendation (amends decision-sheet rec 1):** ε = max(ε_A, ε_N6), where
- ε_A = the 95th bootstrap percentile of the statistic between pairs of stage-2 ingredients (1B, 7B: 3 pairs; 13B: 6 pairs), using the same paired draw;
- ε_N6 = the same between consecutive RL step branches;
- the template/entity bootstrap gives the confidence interval of each base-vs-stage statistic.

### 3.3 Why the primary null is N4′, not ε (T1 on stand-in, `sim3`, `sim4`)

Davis–Kahan: sin Θ ≤ ‖E‖/δ_gap, so **any** small perturbation leaves the low band nearly intact (pre-mortem §1.2). An absolute ε cannot separate "protected" from "small". The pre-mortem's N4 (permute the rows of D among entities) keeps the displacement's size and shape and destroys only which entity got which change. Run on the stand-in at matched ‖D‖/‖H‖ = ρ, 150 permutations:

| Displacement type | f_low(D) percentile vs N4 | d_O percentile vs N4 | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| red: linear map H·A (literal "metric change") | 1.00 (red) | 0.04 / 0.007 (looks **protected**) | d_O false-positive |
| white iid | 0.34 / 0.007 | 0.12 / 0.83 | null-like |
| blue: graph high-pass L²Z | 0.007 (blue) | 0.89 / 0.13 | d_O misses it |
| rewiring S[π] − S (topology genuinely changed) | 1.00 (red) | 0.04 / 0.09 (looks **protected**) | d_O false-positive |

So plain N4 + d_O calls a linear map and a rewiring "protected", because their smooth part (here the −S term) is free under d_O while the permuted copy is not. **Fix (this design):** split off the linear-in-H part first and permute only the residual, keeping D_lin fixed (**N4′**). Under N4′ (`sim4`, r = 22): the linear map reads d_O 0.42/0.33 (null, correct), white 0.85/0.52 (null, correct), blue f_low 0.007 (blue, correct). The rewiring still reads d_O 0.007 at ρ = 0.2: a smooth remainder outside the top-22 PCs survives. f_low(R) flags it red (1.00) at both ρ. Hence the rule: **only f_low(R) vs N4′ votes**; d_O vs N4′ is reported and must agree in direction before any "protected" wording is used.

### 3.4 Exact procedure

1. **Extraction once per checkpoint**: K = 8 per-template vectors for all n entities, all four layers.
2. **Point estimates** use all templates and all entities.
3. **Permutation null N4′**: P = 999 permutations of the rows of R (residual after removing D_lin, re-orthogonalised to P_r after permuting); statistic recomputed with D_lin + R_π. One-sided p-values p_blue = (1 + #{T_π ≤ T_obs})/(1 + P) and p_red = (1 + #{T_π ≥ T_obs})/(1 + P). Minimum attainable p = 0.001.
4. **Bootstrap, B = 1000**: each replicate draws a template multiset (K of K with replacement) **and** an 80% entity subsample without replacement (duplicates would put zero-distance pairs into the kNN and kernel graphs). The same draw is applied to base, every stage, every ingredient and every null; m, r and ℓ* stay frozen. Report percentile 95% CIs of every statistic, and the **verdict stability**: the fraction of replicates where the colour verdict (blue/white/red at α) matches the point verdict. A verdict is claimed only if stability ≥ 0.9.
5. **Legacy P1 decision (decision sheet, kept for continuity):** at stage s and measure M, "within ε" if the upper 95% bootstrap bound of M(base, s) < ε; "beyond ε" if the lower bound > ε; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE. These verdicts are reported, never decisive (§3.3).

### 3.6 Multiple comparisons

- **Primary family per model** = the colour test f_low(R) vs N4′ at ℓ* for each preregistered stage contrast: OLMo-2-0425-1B {SFT, DPO, RLVR-final (Instruct)} vs base, plus Olmo-3 {RL-Zero-Math} vs base.
